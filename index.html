About Our Bathtub Reglazing

Bathtub reglazing is the process of cleaning, repairing and recoating your existing tub so it looks new again. Instead of ripping out a tub that is structurally sound, we bring the surface back to life in a fraction of the time and at a much lower cost than replacement.

Our process is simple and thorough. We start by deep cleaning the tub and removing soap scum, rust and old caulk. Next we repair chips, scratches and worn spots, then etch the surface so the new coating bonds properly. Finally, we apply a durable, high-gloss finish in thin, even coats and let it cure.

Most bathtub reglazing jobs are finished in a single day, and your bathroom is usually ready for use within a short curing window. That means less disruption for your family, tenants or guests. We also work with different tub materials, including porcelain, cast iron, steel and fiberglass.

Whether you want to refresh an old pink or avocado tub, cover years of stains or switch to a clean white finish before listing a property, our reglazing delivers a polished result you can see and feel.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Reglazing

What is bathtub reglazing?

Bathtub reglazing is a restoration process where we clean, repair and apply a new protective coating to your existing tub. It removes the look of stains, dullness and minor damage, and leaves the tub with a smooth, glossy surface.

How long does bathtub reglazing take?

Most tubs can be reglazed in a single visit. After the work is done, the finish needs time to cure before you use the tub. We will give you clear instructions on timing when we schedule your appointment.

Is reglazing cheaper than replacing a bathtub?

In most cases, yes. Reglazing avoids demolition, plumbing work, tile repair and disposal costs, so it is usually a much more affordable way to get a like-new tub. Call us for a free quote based on your bathroom.

How long does a reglazed tub last, and how do I care for it?

With proper care, a professionally reglazed tub can last for many years. Clean it with non-abrasive cleaners and a soft sponge, and avoid harsh chemicals and scouring pads. We will go over simple care tips after your service.

Can you reglaze fiberglass, porcelain and cast iron tubs?

Yes. We work on porcelain, cast iron, steel and fiberglass tubs. If your tub has cracks or chips, our fiberglass tub repair and surface repair steps come first, so the final finish is smooth and even.
